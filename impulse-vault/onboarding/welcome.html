IMPULSE VAULT

One more thought, before you buy.

Right at the moment an impulse buy happens — when you press the checkout button — this is your own self-control tool that gently pauses you. It doesn't block. It just makes a little room to think.

🔒 100% local, on your device only

  • Your searches, products, and stats are never sent to a server.
  • This extension makes no network requests carrying your data.
  • Everything is stored only in chrome.storage.local.
  • You can delete all data at once from settings anytime.

Works only on these sites

Until you grant permission, the extension does nothing at all.

What does it look at?

  • The search terms on results pages (to detect repeated or similar searches)
  • The product name and price on product pages (only when possible, estimated)
  • Whether you've viewed the same product multiple times
  • Buy-button clicks — the moment you really decide

Please grant permission yourself

When you press the button below, the browser opens a permission prompt. Only after you approve there will the extension start working. If you'd rather not, you can decline or turn it off later anytime.
