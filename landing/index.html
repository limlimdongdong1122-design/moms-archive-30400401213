How it works

Three steps. All automatic.

01

Detect

It detects the moment you hit a buy or checkout button on shopping, service, and subscription sites — and reads the price, specs, rating, and reviews right off that page.

02

Clear-eyed analysis

Pros and cons weighed 50:50, plus opportunity cost and alternatives. Turn AI on and it searches the web itself for outside reviews and cheaper or better options.

03

Decide

Buy it anyway, vault it to cool off for 24 hours, or let it go. Every time you hold back, your savings add up. The choice is always yours.

Features

Light, but thorough.

Clear-eyed purchase analysis

Evidence-based pros and cons, an “is this the best choice?” verdict, and a confidence level. It never makes up facts.

AI web-search for alternatives

One click and the AI scours the web for cheaper or better real products, with sources.

Cooling-off Vault

When you feel the urge, drop it in the Vault to cool off. If you still want it in 24 hours, buy it then.

100% local privacy

Searches, products, and stats stay on your device only. Zero requests carry your data to a server.

Impulse profile dashboard

Savings you can see

Every time you hold back, your “Total Saved” grows. Restraint, rewarded in numbers.
