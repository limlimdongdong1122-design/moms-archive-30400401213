Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

The short version: IMPULSE VAULT is built to be private. The browser extension does all of its analysis on your device. We don't run trackers, we don't sell data, and we never collect your browsing or purchase history. The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose to use the optional AI feature, or if you give us your email.

1. The browser extension (100% local)

  • Your settings, impulse stats, vault items, streaks, and savings goal are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local. They never leave your device — except for the optional AI feature described below.
  • The extension only runs on the sites you choose, and only after you grant permission for them.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers.
  • You can wipe everything anytime with the “Delete all my data” button, or by uninstalling.

2. Optional AI analysis

  • Bring-your-own-key (free): if you add your own AI provider key, only the details of the single item you’re deciding on (e.g., title, price, specs) are sent directly to that provider to generate the analysis. Your key and that data go only to the provider you chose.
  • Managed AI (Pro): the single item’s details are sent to our processing endpoint (a Cloudflare Worker) and on to the AI provider to generate the analysis, which is returned to you. We do not send your browsing history, and we don’t retain the content of these requests beyond running them. Basic, non-identifying counters (e.g., per-day request counts) may be kept briefly to prevent abuse.

3. Payments (Pro)

  • Payments are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your card details.
  • After payment you receive a license key. It’s stored locally on your device and verified by our Worker, which checks a cryptographic signature — it does not store personal data.

4. Website & email (lead forms, waitlist, newsletter)

  • If you submit your email (e.g., through an ad lead form, waitlist, or newsletter), we use it only to send your install link and occasional product updates. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • We do not sell or share your email with anyone.
  • Our website may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and basic request info) for security and reliability, as is normal for web hosting.

5. What we never do

  • Sell your data. Share it with advertisers. Track your browsing. Build an ad profile on you.

6. Data retention & your rights

  • Extension data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall.
  • For email, you can unsubscribe or request deletion anytime by contacting us.

7. Children

IMPULSE VAULT is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above.
