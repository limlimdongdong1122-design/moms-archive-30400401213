## PRO FAQ
```
Why is there a paid plan?
The managed AI costs real money to run. Pro keeps it sustainable while the rules-based version stays free forever.

What does the AI actually see?
Only the single product you're deciding on — never your full history. Everything else stays local.

Can I use Pro features for free?
Yes — bring your own AI key (BYOK) in the free version and you get AI analysis with no subscription. Pro just removes the setup.

How do I get my license key?
By email after your PayPal payment. Paste it into the dashboard to activate.

What if I switch computers?
Your key works on multiple devices.
```
